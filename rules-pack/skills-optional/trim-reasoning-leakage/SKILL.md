# Trim Reasoning Leakage

Ask whether a reader at the current revision can resolve every citation and verify every claim without a private plan, chat, review thread, or uncommitted draft. If not, restate the enduring fact from the repository's perspective or delete the passage when it carries no durable proposition.

Remove dead decision labels, review vantage, change narration, self-justifying reviewer prose, control-flow walkthroughs, and unowned planning hedges. Keep committed issue references, measured bounds, suppression reasons, present-tense regression facts, and permitted historical evidence. Do not modernize generated artifacts, snapshots, fixtures, or sealed archived notes.
